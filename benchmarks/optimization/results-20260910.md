# Optimization decisions after the recovered campaign

The authoritative campaign combines original suites 1–16 (900 runs) with recovery
suites 17–26 (308 runs). Exclude the 45 completed rows from interrupted suite 17.
All 26 frozen result verifiers and 36 diagnostic proof-owner audits passed again.
The 1,208 results contain 1,065 optimal outcomes and 143 deadline-capped outcomes.
Complete layout sets and saved solution objects agree across 24 enumeration scopes.

Evidence: `target/optimization-campaign-20260910-recovery/RECOVERY.json` selects the
result locations. `target/optimization-campaign-20260910/analysis` contains the
combined analysis, raw-result hashes, frozen-artifact hashes and verification logs.

## Production decision

Promote descending first-output producer order within each profile. This preserves
every root, profile order, independent proof ownership and cancellation joins.
At 16 total workers, case 10 One min N/L completed in 6/6 candidate runs with a
23.04 s median; every paired baseline reached 120 s. The 8- and 12-worker screens
also changed from no completed baseline runs to complete candidate runs.
Do not turn a timeout into a completion-time speedup ratio.

Reverse ordering added about 0.10 s on case 97 One min N/L at 16 workers. Outside-in
ordering added about 8.74 s on that request with the same observed case-10 benefit,
so reverse is preferred. One-worker execution has no producer partitions; its
ordering is unaffected.

## Next promotion candidate

Static Boolean second-output partitions reduce case 258 All min N/L from 57.06 to
14.74 s (six paired repeats) and case 97 from 452.69 to 218.02 s (two paired repeats,
813 identical layouts). They add about 0.96 s on case 24 and 1.91 s on case 36 at
32 workers. These penalties are acceptable under the user's hard-case priority.
Confirm case 97 and measure the combined descending-order/partition policy before
promoting partitions. Case 10 All min N/L still reaches the 600 s cap.

Adaptive Boolean partitioning remains a follow-up: it helps case 258 at 8 and 16
workers, where static splitting gives little benefit, and reduces small-request
penalties. Its timing evidence has two repeats and case 97 was capped at 180 s.
The 250 ms sparse startup delay is secondary; validate combinations separately.

## Current benchmark requirements

- Prioritize substantial reductions in exact terminal time on the hardest cases.
  Reasonable easy-case penalties are acceptable, up to roughly 10 additional
  seconds. This is a ceiling, not a target or an allowance for every regression.
- Run future performance benchmarks only at 8 or more total solver workers.
  Performance below 8 workers must not block promotion. Exactness, completeness
  and cancellation contracts still apply to supported execution settings.
- The next benchmark session must not exceed three hours, including cleanup and
  verification. Freeze a bounded queue, retain capped outcomes, notify on finish
  or failure, and end the agent turn after launch.
- Use matched pairs and full exact results. Historical cross-session baseline
  medians, root counts and partial enumeration counts are not speedup evidence.
